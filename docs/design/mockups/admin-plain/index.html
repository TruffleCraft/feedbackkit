Plain admin, three variants

Each frame is the real clickable mock, scaled down. Open a variant to click through every screen, resize the window to see the phone layout, and switch light and dark. All names and numbers are invented.

Theme

A · Top bar and tabs

Closest to today's structure. A text navigation, project sections as tabs, one list at a time, a report opens as its own page. Open the clickable mock

B · Sidebar and split view

The pattern of NORA Web and the iCloud-MCP dashboard. Projects and their sections live in a sidebar, reports show list and detail side by side from 1180 px. Open the clickable mock

C · Drill-down lists

The iOS Settings pattern. Every level is one grouped list and one question, nothing else on the screen. Quietest, most clicks. Open the clickable mock
